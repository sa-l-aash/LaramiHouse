Larami Houses

Larami Houses is a hospitality brand built around simplicity, comfort, and proximity to nature. One home sits in Narok town, designed for ease and rest. The other sits closer to the Maasai Mara ecosystem, designed for immersion and silence.

Together, they offer travelers a bridge between modern comfort and wild landscapes.
