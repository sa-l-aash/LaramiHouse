WELCOME TO LARAMI HOUSES

Thoughtfully Curated Stays Between Town and Wilderness

Larami Houses offers two distinct experiences in Kenya's Narok region. Whether you're looking for a comfortable stay in town or a secluded retreat on the edge of the Maasai Mara ecosystem, each property is designed to provide comfort, privacy, and a meaningful connection to its surroundings.

From modern self-contained accommodation in Narok to an eco-friendly wilderness sanctuary in the Mara, every stay is carefully prepared to help guests slow down, explore, and feel at home.
