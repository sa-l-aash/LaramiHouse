About Mara House

Larami House Mara is an eco-inspired retreat located near Maasai Mara conservancies. It offers a peaceful connection to wildlife, open landscapes, and natural silence.

Experience includes

  • Eco-friendly accommodation powered by solar energy
  • Close proximity to wildlife corridors
  • Peaceful natural surroundings with open views
  • Guided game drive options (extra service)
  • Ideal for nature lovers, photographers, and explorers
